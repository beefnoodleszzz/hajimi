# GPT-Image-2 Visual Prompts

> Use case: editorial science explainer. Generate still keyframes only; add all readable text, arrows, equations and labels in post. Start with `quality: medium`; only upgrade the selected hero frame after composition passes.

## Shared visual constraints

```text
No text, no labels, no arrows, no logos, no watermark, no fake news footage, no recognizable real person, no gore, scientifically grounded proportions, clear subject separation, vertical 9:16 composition, leave clean negative space for post-production captions.
```

## A — Hook: Earth under a sudden stop

```text
Scene: low Earth orbit, black space, the planet filling the lower two-thirds of a vertical frame, a thin blue atmospheric rim and a small cloud belt visible along the curve.
Subject: Earth viewed from a three-quarter angle with the equatorial region facing the viewer; the surface is visually stable while a few extremely subtle eastward dust-like streaks sit just above the atmosphere.
Key details: controlled natural contrast, dark navy space, cool cyan atmospheric edge, one restrained amber cluster of city lights, calm geometry with a clear sense of scale, editorial scientific illustration rather than movie poster art.
Use case: opening keyframe for a vertical educational short; leave the upper third clean for captions.
Constraints: no disaster explosion, no giant cracks, no exaggerated fire, no fantasy continents, no text or symbols, no extra planets, preserve an anatomically plausible Earth shape.
```

## B — Ground stops, loose matter keeps moving

```text
Scene: a simplified equatorial city edge at dusk, viewed from street level, wide empty roadway and a row of glass-and-concrete buildings.
Subject: the pavement and buildings form a stable horizontal base while loose paper, fine dust, and a few cloud wisps are visibly stretched toward screen right, implying eastward inertia.
Key details: hard side light from screen left, crisp shadows on the pavement, transparent dust particles, rough asphalt texture, a single cyan velocity trace added only as a subtle light streak with no arrowhead.
Use case: visual explanation of relative motion, designed for a slow lateral parallax move in a vertical short.
Constraints: no identifiable people, no traffic, no wreckage, no tornado, no tsunami, no text, no logos, keep the buildings vertical and the pavement level.
```

## C — Atmosphere and ocean retain motion

```text
Scene: elevated view over a coastal city and adjacent ocean, late afternoon overcast daylight, coastline running diagonally from lower left to upper right.
Subject: fixed buildings and shoreline below; a coherent cloud deck, sea spray, and long surface ripples lean toward screen right as separate flowing layers.
Key details: overcast diffused daylight, atmospheric haze at the horizon, wet concrete and dark blue water as material anchors, visible separation between land, air, and water layers, restrained scientific visualization.
Use case: middle escalation frame for a vertical explainer; leave lower center open for one short caption.
Constraints: no giant wave, no flooded city, no storm eye, no text, no map labels, no false aerial photography claim, preserve clear layer boundaries.
```

## D — Polar contrast

```text
Scene: a small polar research station on a flat snow field under a dark blue sky, faint aurora near the horizon, vertical 9:16 framing.
Subject: the station and snow surface remain visually calm, with only a thin plume of exhaust rising nearly vertically; the horizon is stable and the composition feels almost motionless compared with the equatorial shots.
Key details: cold blue hour light, frosted metal siding, granular snow texture, one warm window as the only amber accent, soft wind-carved snow ridges as environmental pressure.
Use case: contrast shot explaining why rotational speed depends on latitude.
Constraints: no flags whipping sideways, no blizzard, no people, no text, no fantasy aurora shapes, keep the station small against the planetary-scale environment.
```

## E — Gravity still works

```text
Subject: the soles stay pressed into the ground while a thin veil of dust moves sideways toward screen right; the body above the boots is cropped out so the frame explains contact and inertia rather than identity.

Use case: misconception-correction frame for the line about gravity.
Constraints: no flying body, no floating boots, no footprints leading off frame, no debris storm, no text, no logo, no gore.
```

## F — Restart mismatch as an abstract model

```text
Scene: clean dark background with three separated horizontal layers: a solid land slab at bottom, a translucent air layer above it, and a blue ocean layer between them.
Subject: the land layer is sharply defined and steady while the air and water layers show different eastward streak patterns, like a controlled laboratory diagram rather than a literal disaster scene.

Constraints: no explosion, no city, no people, no text, no arrows, no planet cracking, keep the three layers distinct and physically readable.
```
