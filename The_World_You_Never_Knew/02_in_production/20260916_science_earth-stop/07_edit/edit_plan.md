# Edit Plan

## Timeline

- Canvas: 1080×1920, 9:16
- Frame rate: 30 fps
- Target length: 48–52 seconds
- Codec: H.264 high-quality export
- Audio: 48 kHz AAC

## Assembly order

1. Lock the TTS dry voice and remove only unusable breaths or long pauses。
2. Place the 10 shot blocks against the voice; keep the first visual change before 2 seconds。
3. Add motion graphics for the 465 m/s number, 465 m distance and latitude contrast。
4. Add English captions; emphasize only `STOP`, `465 m/s`, `GRAVITY` and `FIRST`。
5. Add one restrained BGM bed and three SFX accents: stop hit, eastward whoosh, final question resolve。
6. Review once muted and once audio-only before export。

## Motion rules

- One main move per shot: push-in, lateral parallax, or locked graphic reveal。
- Use the cyan eastward trace as the recurring visual motif; do not add arrows to every shot。
- No glitch montage, whip transitions or constant zooms；the information change is the transition。
- Shot 06 must breathe for at least 3 seconds to make the poles-versus-equator contrast legible。

## Subtitle rules

- English only, short lines, high contrast。
- Keep text inside the center safe area；avoid the right-side Shorts controls and bottom description area。
- Do not use word-by-word karaoke for the whole narration。
- Manually verify `Coriolis`, `equator`, all numbers and the em dash pause。

## Audio hierarchy

```text
Voiceover > key SFX > BGM > ambience
```

BGM must never compete with the words. Listen on headphones, Mac speakers and phone speaker before accepting the export。

## V1 acceptance gate

- [ ] First 2 seconds show the exact premise。
- [ ] Numeric claims appear only after the narration introduces them。
- [ ] `465 m/s` is clearly marked as equatorial maximum / example。
- [ ] “simplified model” appears before the 465 m displacement claim。
- [ ] Gravity misconception is corrected without introducing a new unsupported claim。
- [ ] All AI-generated scientific visuals are disclosed in the description。
- [ ] Muted playback still shows the land / air / water logic。
- [ ] Audio-only playback still contains a complete understandable story。
