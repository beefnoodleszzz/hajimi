# Shorts Script V2 — Story Rebuild

## Editorial decision

这版不再把多个知识点并列展示，而是只讲一个可视化事件：**赤道附近的地面突然停止一秒，而地面以上的运动继续向东。**

- 删除：极地站、Coriolis 单独解释、固定靴子镜头、无因果的 Earth-from-orbit 收尾。
- 保留：465 m/s、约 465 m 相对位移、重力仍然存在、空气和海洋的惯性、重启时的错位。
- 视觉主线：同一条城市道路 + 一个红色松动物体，局部运动逐步扩展到空气、海洋和地球系统。
- 02 号声音继续作为音色基线，但按本稿重新生成旁白；不再拉伸旧录音去适配镜头。

## Logline

一条赤道城市道路突然锁死；一个人和一张红色收据继续向东，随后观众才意识到，空气和海洋也没有停下来。

## Voiceover V2

> Imagine the ground under your feet stopping for exactly one second. At the equator, you are already moving east at about four hundred sixty-five meters per second. The road stops — but your body keeps going. In that single second, you move nearly four hundred sixty-five meters relative to the ground: about four football fields, without taking a step. Gravity still holds you down. The danger is sideways. The air, clouds, and ocean keep their momentum too. When the ground starts again, land, air, water, and everything loose are no longer aligned. That mismatch is the real disaster. What moves first: you, the atmosphere, or the ocean?

- Word count: 103
- Expected duration: approximately 44–52 seconds; final cut follows the actual selected take's ASR timestamps.
- Assumption marker: `Imagine` makes the magical instantaneous-stop premise explicit without spending a separate exposition block.
- Scientific guardrail: the 465 m/s and 465 m figures are presented as the equatorial simplified model, not a universal value for every latitude.

## Beat architecture

| Beat | Function | Visual change |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Ground locks | Hook and premise | A normal street freezes mid-motion |
| 2. You continue | Personal consequence | The body and red receipt move east while the road stays fixed |
| 3. Make it physical | Scale | The same eastward trajectory becomes 465 m across city blocks |
| 4. Expand the system | Escalation | Air, cloud band, and ocean carry the same momentum |
| 5. Restart mismatch | Payoff | Land, air, water lurch out of alignment |
| 6. Gravity / sideways | Correct the mental image | Downward gravity remains; the dangerous vector is horizontal |
| 7. Callback question | Comment hook | Return to the same road and ask what moves first |
