# EP001 Beat Script v01

> 38-second animatic script. Each sentence carries one punch. The stop is
> explicitly framed as a magical thought experiment.

## BEAT 01 — 0.0–1.2

- voice: `Imagine the ground under your feet stopping for exactly one second.`
- visual: normal road locks; receipt begins to slide right.
- sound: vacuum drop + hard impact.
- purpose: anomaly before context.

## BEAT 02 — 1.2–4.0

- voice: `The ground stops. You don't.`
- visual: same silhouette and red receipt continue east while road markings stay fixed.
- sound: body whoosh, paper tick.
- purpose: personal consequence.

## BEAT 03 — 4.0–7.0

- voice: `At the equator, you are already moving east at about four hundred sixty-five meters per second.`
- visual: `465 m/s` replaces the street; a cyan vector points right.
- sound: rising tonal marker.
- purpose: scale setup.

## BEAT 04 — 7.0–11.0

- voice: `In one second, that is roughly four hundred sixty-five meters relative to the ground.`
- visual: line draws across a city grid and lands on `465 m`.
- sound: distance sweep + click.
- purpose: turn speed into distance.

## BEAT 05 — 11.0–16.0

- voice: `Your body, the air, and the clouds keep that sideways motion too.`
- visual: city slab stays fixed while an air band moves right.
- sound: air rush.
- purpose: expand from human to atmosphere.

## BEAT 06 — 16.0–21.0

- voice: `The ocean does not politely stop with the pavement.`
- visual: ocean layer joins the cyan eastward trace.
- sound: low water surge.
- purpose: escalate the system.

## BEAT 07 — 21.0–27.0

- voice: `When the ground starts again, land, air, and water are no longer aligned.`
- visual: deterministic three-layer mismatch; S007 is the hero shot.
- sound: restart hit + layered lag.
- purpose: payoff.

## BEAT 08 — 27.0–31.0

- voice: `Gravity still holds you down. The danger is sideways.`
- visual: down arrow stays attached; right arrow dominates.
- sound: low gravity thump, then silence gap.
- purpose: correct the fly-into-space misconception.

## BEAT 09 — 31.0–35.0

- voice: `That is the real disaster: a one-second mismatch in motion.`
- visual: layers compress to one phrase: `MOTION ≠ GROUND`.
- sound: restrained sub drop.
- purpose: name the thesis.

## BEAT 10 — 35.0–38.0

- voice: `One second. Four hundred sixty-five meters. What moves first?`
- visual: return to the opening road; receipt is now far right.
- sound: dust settle + loop tail.
- purpose: earned comment hook and visual loop.

## Full temporary voiceover

Imagine the ground under your feet stopping for exactly one second. The ground
stops. You don't. At the equator, you are already moving east at about four
hundred sixty-five meters per second. In one second, that is roughly four
hundred sixty-five meters relative to the ground. Your body, the air, and the
clouds keep that sideways motion too. The ocean does not politely stop with the
pavement. When the ground starts again, land, air, and water are no longer
aligned. Gravity still holds you down. The danger is sideways. That is the real
disaster: a one-second mismatch in motion. One second. Four hundred sixty-five
meters. What moves first?
