# EP001 Fact Pack

Research basis retained from the previous project only as verified facts and
sources. No old wording, shot list, subtitle, voice, or render is reused.

## Confirmed facts

- Earth rotates once per solar day; the sidereal period is about 23 h 56 min.
- NASA GSFC gives an equatorial surface speed of about 465 m/s.
- In the instantaneous-stop thought experiment, unattached objects retain
  eastward tangential motion and the speed depends on latitude.
- Gravity remains; equatorial tangential speed is not enough to launch people
  into space.
- Earth’s rotation affects the direction of moving air and ocean currents via
  the Coriolis effect.

## Simplified derivations

- At the equator, `d ≈ v × t ≈ 465 m/s × 1 s ≈ 465 m`.
- In a spherical approximation, `v(latitude) ≈ 465 × cos(latitude) m/s`.
- “Land, air, and water are no longer aligned after restart” is an inference,
  not a numerical disaster simulation.

## Rejected claims

The script does not claim that everybody moves at 1,000 mph, flies into space,
creates a quantified mega-tsunami, makes the atmosphere disappear, or causes
Earth to explode.
