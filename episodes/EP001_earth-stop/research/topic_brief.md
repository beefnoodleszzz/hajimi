# EP001 Topic Brief

## Question

What would happen if Earth’s rotation stopped for exactly one second?

## Counter-intuitive point

The road can be imagined to stop while a person, loose objects, air, and water
retain eastward tangential motion.

## Payoff

At the equator, the simplified first-order model gives roughly 465 metres of
relative movement in one second. Gravity remains; the dangerous vector is
sideways.

## Visual peaks

1. The ground locks before the explanation begins.
2. `465 m/s` becomes a visible `465 m` trajectory.
3. Land, air, and water restart out of alignment.

## Shorts fit

Strong fit: the thought experiment has an immediate anomaly, a personal
consequence, a scale reveal, and a loopable callback. It can expand into a
long-form episode about angular momentum, atmosphere, oceans, and latitude.

## Guardrail

This is a magical instantaneous-stop thought experiment, not a real mechanism
or a full geophysical simulation. Claims, inferences, and simplifications are
kept separate in `fact_pack.md`.
