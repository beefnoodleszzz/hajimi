# EP001 Creative Brief

## Emotional curve

Normality → snap → personal danger → scale awe → system escalation →
catastrophic mismatch → physical correction → looped question.

## Viewer expectation

The first frame implies a familiar street video. The audience expects a normal
physics explanation; the locked road violates that expectation immediately.

## Visual thesis

One eastward vector is the protagonist. It starts as a red receipt, becomes a
human-scale displacement, then crosses city blocks and finally separates land,
air, and water.

## One-frame memory point

The hero frame is S007: three clean horizontal layers labelled `LAND`, `AIR`,
and `OCEAN`, with land restarting left and the upper layers visibly offset to
the right.

## Deterministic backbone

S003, S004, S005, S006, S007, and S008 require exact vectors, labels, scale,
and layer relationships; they are Fusion/Blender territory. AI video is
allowed only for the human inertia plate in S002 and must preserve the same
rightward direction and red receipt.

## Forbidden shortcut

No beat may become a static scenic plate with a narration paragraph laid over
it. Each beat has an action or a measurable graphical change.
