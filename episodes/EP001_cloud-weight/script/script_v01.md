# EP001 — Cloud Weight

Language: en-US
Target: 35.5 seconds
Status: draft for animatic review
Source: U.S. Geological Survey cloud-weight estimate; see `research/fact_pack.md`.

## Voiceover

**0.0–1.4 — HOOK**
That cloud weighs five hundred fifty tons.

**1.4–4.6 — DEFINE THE NUMBER**
One cubic-kilometre cumulus: five hundred fifty-one tons.

**4.6–8.6 — MATERIAL REVEAL**
Liquid water—tiny droplets, spread wide.

**8.6–12.6 — DENSITY TURN**
Why doesn't it fall? Cloudy air is less dense than dry air.

**12.6–17.6 — MOTION**
Rising air carries the droplets up.

**17.6–24.0 — HERO EXPLANATION**
Heavy water, riding inside moving air.

**24.0–30.0 — PAYOFF**
When drops outgrow the updraft, they fall as rain.

**30.0–35.5 — LOOP**
A cloud can be heavy—and still float.

## Performance direction

Curious and controlled, with a clean stop on “five hundred fifty tons,” a more explanatory middle, and a warm, certain landing on “still float.” The temporary animatic voice is disposable; production narration must be an authorized local VoxCPM2 take selected per beat.
