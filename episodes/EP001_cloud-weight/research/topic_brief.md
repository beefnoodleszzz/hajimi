# Topic brief — EP001 cloud weight

## Question

How can a visible cloud contain hundreds of tons of water and still remain in the sky?

## Chosen angle

Put a cloud on a weighing scale before explaining anything. Then reveal that the number is an estimate for a specified one-kilometre-cube cumulus cloud, and that “floating” is a density relationship rather than weightlessness.

## Audience promise

In 35.5 seconds, the viewer sees the apparent contradiction, the water-droplet estimate, the density relationship, and the updraft/rain payoff.

## Production constraints

- The first anomaly must be readable without narration in 1.5 seconds.
- The explanatory visuals must be deterministic enough for AI image/Flow/Fusion.
- S006 is the hero cutaway: cloud layer, liquid droplets, and upward air motion in one readable composition.
- Temporary animatic speech is not production narration. Production voice must be generated by the local authorized VoxCPM2 workflow.
