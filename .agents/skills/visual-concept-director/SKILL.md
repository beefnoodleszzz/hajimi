---

# Objective

Design visual language, hero frames, and shot-level visual actions that carry
the current story on mute. Read creative direction and beat intent; do not
reuse a legacy shot list by default.

# Required output

Record thesis, motif, scale/movement/lighting/color language, foreground,
midground, background, focal subject, camera, action, transition, and AI/Fusion
method candidates for every shot. Keep the composition cinematic first; exact
scientific labels are a later Fusion responsibility.

# Reject

Reject decorative plates, narration-only coverage, repeated camera logic,
subtitle-dependent frames, and complex compositions without a clear focal relationship.
