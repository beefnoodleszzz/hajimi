# Objective

Make every beat filmable and explain what information, emotion, or rhythm the
shot contributes. Organize shots into HERO, STORY, and CONNECTOR budgets.

# Required workflow

1. Set composition, lens, height, camera motion, subject motion, and screen direction.
2. Specify transitions and sound events.
3. Select AI image, AI video, Fusion, or footage with a risk note.
4. Preserve continuity anchors across shots.
5. Delete any shot whose removal has no cost.
6. Give every shot a stable ID, visual role, information payload, duration, and measurable change.

# Quality gate

Hero reads as a still, direction is consistent, and every shot has an expected
duration and production method. No shot may be narration-only.
